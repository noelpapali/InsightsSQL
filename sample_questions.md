# Sample Questions

A range of questions you can ask the chatbot, from simple lookups to complex multi-table analysis.

## Simple

- What are the total sales this year?
- How many unique customers do we have?
- What is our most sold product by units?
- What is the total profit across all time?
- How many sales transactions were there last month?

## Medium

- Who are the top 5 salespersons by revenue this year?
- Show me monthly revenue for 2016.
- What is the average order value by product category?
- Which customers have spent the most with us in the last 12 months?
- Which products have generated the most profit?

## Complex

- Which salesperson had the highest profit margin last year?
- Compare total revenue between Q1 and Q2 of 2016.
- What are the top 3 products for each buying group?
- Which customers haven't placed an order this year?
- Show sales performance by salesperson broken down by quarter for 2016.
